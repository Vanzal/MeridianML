Magic LLM – logo concepts

Bold, geometric, monochrome (Vercel/Linear). The first three are mixes of Token M and Causal cursor; below them are all five original concepts. Each is shown at 16, 32, 64 and 128 px, light and dark, as a browser-tab icon and in the navigation bar. All marks use currentColor, so they follow any theme.
